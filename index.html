Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What's new in NovaFlow 2.0 vs the original?+
NovaFlow 2.0 adds a second wavelength: blue light (450nm) studied for antibacterial activity in nasal tissue — something red light alone can't address. The device runs two separate 10-minute modes (blue first, then red) for 20 minutes total. Same device, complete approach.
I can't stop using nasal spray. Can NovaFlow help?+
This is one of the most common reasons people find us. Sprays like Afrin cause rebound congestion (rhinitis medicamentosa) after just a few days of use — your tissue becomes dependent on them just to stay open. NovaFlow uses light therapy with no chemicals and nothing to become dependent on. Many users have stepped off spray dependency with it. We recommend working with your doctor during the transition.
I wake up congested every morning. Will this fix it?+
Morning congestion is one of the most common things our users report improving. Nasal tissue swells overnight when you're lying flat — red light supports the tissue's ability to manage that swelling, and blue light targets bacterial buildup that compounds it. Most users see improvement within 2–4 weeks of consistent use. Results vary.
My congestion is from allergies. Will this still work?+
Many of our users have allergy-driven congestion. Red light supports the nasal tissue's ability to reduce inflammation regardless of what triggered it. Blue light addresses the bacterial environment that often worsens during allergy season. It won't stop your immune response to allergens, but many allergy sufferers report significantly less congestion day-to-day.
I've tried everything. Why would this be different?+
Most solutions treat the symptom — sprays constrict blood vessels, antihistamines block histamine, steroid sprays reduce surface inflammation. NovaFlow works at the tissue level through light, not chemistry. It's a different mechanism entirely, which is why people who've tried everything else often find it's the first thing that actually helps long-term. The 120-day guarantee means you can find out for yourself at zero risk.
I can't sleep because I can't breathe through my nose. Will this help?+
Sleep-disrupting congestion is one of the primary use cases for NovaFlow. Nasal congestion worsens when you lie flat and gravity redistributes blood flow to nasal tissue. A session before bed — or a consistent daily routine — helps many users wake up breathing clearly for the first time in years. Results vary by individual.
Can I breathe normally while using it?+
Yes. The silicone tips rest gently inside your nostrils. They don't create a seal or block airflow — you breathe completely normally during every session.
Can it be used with a deviated septum?+
Yes. It won't correct the structural issue, but many users with a deviated septum report meaningful relief from the congestion that comes with it.
Will this fit smaller nostrils?+
Yes. NovaFlow 2.0 comes with soft silicone sleeves. If they feel too large, you can use the device without them for a smaller, more comfortable fit.

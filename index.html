Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What's new in NovaFlow 2.0 vs the original?+
NovaFlow 2.0 adds one entirely new mode. Mode 1 is the same clinically studied 660nm red light the original is known for — for surface-level symptoms like allergies and everyday congestion. Mode 2 is what’s new: 2 minutes of antibacterial blue light automatically transitions into 8 minutes of red light, 10 minutes total, to clear bacteria from the nasal lining first and then support tissue recovery. No other home device sequences the lights for you.
I can't stop using nasal spray. Can NovaFlow help?+
This is one of the most common reasons people find us. Sprays like Afrin cause rebound congestion (rhinitis medicamentosa) after just a few days of use — your tissue becomes dependent on them just to stay open. NovaFlow uses light therapy with no chemicals and nothing to become dependent on. Many users have stepped off spray dependency with it. We recommend working with your doctor during the transition.
I wake up congested every morning. Will this fix it?+
Morning congestion is one of the most common things our users report improving. Nasal tissue swells overnight when you're lying flat. A session before bed using Mode 2 — the blue-to-red auto-sequence — clears bacterial buildup first, then supports tissue recovery so you wake up with less congestion. Most users see improvement within 2–4 weeks of consistent use. Results vary.
My congestion is from allergies. Will this still work?+
Many of our users have allergy-driven congestion. Mode 1 — 10 minutes of 660nm red light — is designed specifically for surface-level symptoms like this. It supports your nasal tissue's ability to manage inflammation regardless of what triggered it. It won't stop your immune response to allergens, but many allergy sufferers report significantly less congestion day-to-day.
I've tried everything. Why would this be different?+
Most solutions treat the symptom — sprays constrict blood vessels, antihistamines block histamine, steroid sprays reduce surface inflammation. NovaFlow works at the tissue level through light, not chemistry. It's a different mechanism entirely, which is why people who've tried everything else often find it's the first thing that actually helps long-term. The 120-day guarantee means you can find out for yourself at zero risk.
I can't sleep because I can't breathe through my nose. Will this help?+
Sleep-disrupting congestion is one of the primary use cases for NovaFlow. Nasal congestion worsens when you lie flat and gravity redistributes blood flow to nasal tissue. A Mode 2 session before bed — the 10-minute blue-to-red auto-sequence — helps many users wake up breathing clearly for the first time in years. Results vary by individual.
Can I breathe normally while using it?+
Yes. The silicone tips rest gently inside your nostrils. They don't create a seal or block airflow — you breathe completely normally during every session.
Can it be used with a deviated septum?+
Yes. It won't correct the structural issue, but many users with a deviated septum report meaningful relief from the congestion that comes with it.
Is red light therapy safe?+
While red light therapy is widely regarded as a gentle, low-risk option for managing discomfort, it is not a substitute for standard medical care. Sinus issues can stem from a variety of underlying causes — including bacterial infections, structural blockages, or severe allergies — that light therapy may not cure on its own. If your sinus symptoms are severe, persistent, or accompanied by fever and acute pain, it's essential to consult a healthcare professional. A doctor can provide an accurate diagnosis, rule out potential complications, and ensure you are treating the root cause effectively.
Will this fit smaller nostrils?+
Yes. NovaFlow 2.0 comes with soft silicone sleeves. If they feel too large, you can use the device without them for a smaller, more comfortable fit.
